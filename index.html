Common questions

How do I play?
Each morning, check the forecast, buy supplies, and set your recipe and price. Then open the stand and watch your customers' thought bubbles. Tap the ? button in the game for a full guide.

Why did my drink change?
The menu follows the seasons: lemonade in spring and summer, apple cider in fall, and hot cocoa in winter. Each new season needs new ingredients.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.

Does the game have ads or in-app purchases?
No. There are no ads, purchases, or accounts.
