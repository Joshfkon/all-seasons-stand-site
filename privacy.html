Privacy Policy

All Seasons Stand · Last updated October 25, 2026

The short version

All Seasons Stand does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Ever.

What the app stores

Your game progress (cash, inventory, recipes, upgrades, and settings) is saved only on your device. It never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers
  • No ads and no in-app purchases
  • No internet connection is used by the game
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
